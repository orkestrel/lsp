## Items

1. **Generation-owned `close()`.** The child reference is retained until termination settles.
   A `start()` issued while a `close()` is in flight is refused `duplicate` (the first child
   is still live). A concurrent `close()` awaits the same settlement rather than resolving
   early on the cleared field. When `stopChild` reports `false`, the reference is retained and
   `close()` rejects with a coded `LSPError` naming the still-live child. Red-first per seam:
   the start-during-close row and the concurrent-close row are recorded red at the baseline
   before the fix.
2. **Generation-scoped emission.** A generation's listeners stop reaching the shared emitter
   after that generation's `close()` has resolved, and never reach a replacement generation:
   no stale `exit`, no stale `chunk`. Constraints: the `exit` event still emits at most once
   for a generation that terminates unprompted; and `close()` must never await stream close
   unboundedly — a pipe-holding grandchild would hang it — so any wait it adds stays bounded
   by `grace`. The mechanism (detach at settlement, a generation guard on each emit, or
   another shape) is your recorded choice within those constraints. The binding row: a
   fixture child that spawns a detached grandchild inheriting its stdout and exits; after
   `close()` resolves and a replacement `start()`, the replacement generation observes no
   stale `exit` or `chunk` when the grandchild later releases the pipe. Record the row red at
   the baseline.
3. **`buildSpawn` adoption.** `#launch` composes a `ProcessCommand` from the positional
   `command` array and spawns `buildSpawn`'s returned `SpawnInput`, including `verbatim` as
   `windowsVerbatimArguments`. The public `StdioTransportOptions` type is unchanged — the
   reconciliation records that decision.
4. **Contract prose.** The transport contract states the generation obligation where a foreign
   implementer meets it: the `exit` event is emitted only for the transport's current
   generation — on the `LSPTransportEventMap` `exit` entry and the `LSPTransportInterface`
   remarks in `src/core/types.ts`, TSDoc prose only, no declaration change.
5. **Guide honesty.** The `### StdioTransport` methods table in `guides/lsp.md` is deleted and
   its behavior sentences fold into the `## Stdio transport` prose; re-read the reconnect
   sentence at `guides/lsp.md:82-84` against the repaired close and make it state what ships.
6. **Suite repairs.** The reconnect row gets the one-row mutation account (refuse `start`
   whenever `#child !== undefined` — that mutation reddens the reconnect row alone). The
   Oxlint integration row's client teardown and pid reaping move into `finally`. The `setup`
   project registers in `vite.config.ts` with a root `tests/setupServer.test.ts` proving
   `readChildProcesses` (the two-snapshot intersection that carries the no-orphan receipt),
   and the `test:setup` script lands per `.claude/rules/workspace.md`.
