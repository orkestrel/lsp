    Make the conformance drift formatter total
    
    The combined audit executed a BigInt through formatConformanceValue and
    read the thrown TypeError: the formatter accepted unknown, called
    JSON.stringify, and documented no failure, so a drift report over an
    unserializable value hid the drift it reported. The adopted arm removes
    the failure mode instead of documenting it: a value JSON.stringify
    cannot serialize formats through String(value). The TSDoc states the
    total behavior, and a red-first BigInt row pins the String form.
