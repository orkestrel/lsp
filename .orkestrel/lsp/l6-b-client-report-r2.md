# L6-B client report successor

This report succeeds [§ What changed](l6-b-client-report.md#1-what-changed) in
`l6-b-client-report.md` and records each converted test row.

| Old title | New title | Adopted contract ruling | Coverage retired |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bounds a push diagnostic publication deadline` | `bounds a push diagnostic publication with its call signal` | The required `LSPOpenOptions.signal` bounds the pushed diagnostics wait. The constructor `timeout` leaves the push and pull diagnostics waits. | The conversion retired coverage that expected the constructor timeout to reject a silent pushed publication with `timeout`. The replacement covers caller-signal rejection with `aborted` while the URI remains owned and closeable. |
| `times out one request and sends its cancel notification` | `aborts one request and sends its cancel notification` | The caller signal reaches the correlated pull-diagnostic request. Diagnostic cancellation rejects with `aborted` and writes `$/cancelRequest` while the generation remains ready. Initialize and shutdown expiry remain lifecycle `timeout` outcomes. | The conversion retired coverage that expected the constructor timeout to reject one pull request and send its cancellation. It was the only row that reached the `#timeoutRequest` cancellation branch. Unit L6.1 deletes that branch under the ruling in `l6-audit-reconciliation.md`, so the retired coverage describes machinery that no longer exists. The replacement covers caller abort and the resulting `$/cancelRequest`. |
| `detaches a drained publication deadline before the next generation` | `detaches a drained publication signal before the next generation` | Publication records retain the caller signal and its listener for deterministic removal at settlement. The constructor timeout no longer creates a diagnostics deadline. | The conversion retired coverage that waited for a drained constructor deadline and proved that its later expiry did not settle the next generation's publication. The replacement aborts the drained generation's caller signal and proves that its detached listener does not settle the next generation's publication. |
