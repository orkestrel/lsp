## Expected

The brief requires these properties to hold together:

- The bare client names `createMCPLegacyClientTransport` only for `server/discover` method-not-found or an initialize result carrying a legacy revision.
- A `server/discover` response with `-32000` `Bad Request: Server not initialized` admits a modern handshake.
- The `/connect` fixture then answers `initialize` with `MCP_PROTOCOL_VERSION`, and `connect()` succeeds after `server/discover`, `initialize`, and `notifications/initialized`.

## Found

The guide and guards classify every supported `initialize` revision as legacy. They define no modern initialize answer. They also state that the bare client sends only `server/discover` and never attempts the legacy handshake. The `/connect` fixture answers `initialize` with `MCP_PROTOCOL_VERSION`, which the guide and `isMCPLegacyVersion` guard classify as legacy evidence. The brief therefore requires the same answer to make `connect()` succeed and make the adapter-naming refusal fire.
