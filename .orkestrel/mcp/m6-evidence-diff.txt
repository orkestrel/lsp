    Rename the version constants to their era-true names
    
    The ruled cascade, taken under the delegated final say and recorded in
    the campaign ledger: SUPPORTED_PROTOCOL_VERSIONS becomes
    SUPPORTED_MODERN_PROTOCOL_VERSIONS, SUPPORTED_CLIENT_PROTOCOL_VERSIONS
    becomes SUPPORTED_MCP_VERSIONS beside its isMCPVersion guard,
    MCP_PROTOCOL_VERSION becomes MCP_HANDSHAKE_VERSION, and
    MCP_LEGACY_VERSION becomes MCP_FALLBACK_VERSION. The MCPLegacy family
    keeps its names, MCP_PROTOCOL_VERSION_HEADER stays untouched as the
    vendor wire header, and tests/mirrors/ stays vendored bytes. No alias,
    re-export, or deprecation tag survives the cascade.
    
    TSDoc sentences the rename left false are repaired in
    src/core/constants.ts: the modern set's superset reading gains its
    qualifier, the combined set names the isMCPVersion guard it feeds
    instead of a client allowlist no surface implements, and the fallback
    constant's claim to the unsupported-revision fallback role is corrected
    to the role the code gives it - the older legacy revision the optional
    legacy decorator accepts and an adapter can pin. The
    unsupported-revision fallback resolves to MCP_HANDSHAKE_VERSION in
    inferLegacyVersion and buildInitializeResult and always did.

    integration 4 passed. A word-boundary grep for each old spelling over
