`MCPListenOptions` sits beside `MCPCallOptions` and `MCPDispatchOptions`; `DEFAULT_MCP_SUBSCRIPTION_CAPACITY` beside `DEFAULT_MCP_REQUEST_TIMEOUT`. The shape reads as the package's own.
