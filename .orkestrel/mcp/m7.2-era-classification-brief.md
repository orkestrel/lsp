`MCP_UNSUPPORTED_VERSION` and `JSONRPC_INVALID_PARAMS` and converts EVERY other failure into the
adapter-naming refusal. An HTTP 401 from a bearer guard, a network fault, and a server-state
refusal of early discovery all misclassify as "legacy peer", which is false guidance and, in the
id-less row, breaks a connect the fixture intends to succeed.

`MCP_PROTOCOL_VERSION`, and the row asserts the request order `server/discover`, `initialize`,
`notifications/initialized` with the connect succeeding.
